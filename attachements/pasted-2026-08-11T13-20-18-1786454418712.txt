Meet Idrissi — Licensed Professional Guide

With over 15 years of experience guiding visitors through Morocco's most captivating cities, landscapes, and cultural heritage, Idrissi brings extensive professional expertise, genuine warmth, and an insider's knowledge that can transform a sightseeing trip into a truly memorable experience.

A licensed professional guide recognized by the Moroccan Ministry of Tourism, Idrissi has also served as Former President of the Tangier Tour Guides Association, reflecting his experience and standing within Morocco's professional tourism community.

Fluent in English, French, Spanish, and Arabic, Idrissi is well placed to welcome travelers from around the world and provide personalized experiences tailored to their interests.

His expertise also includes Jewish heritage tours, helping families and travelers explore Morocco's rich Jewish history, cultural heritage, historic communities, and ancestral connections.

Whether discovering Tangier for the first time, exploring Morocco's cultural treasures, or tracing family roots, guests can expect professional guidance, personal attention, authentic local insight, and a warm Moroccan welcome.
